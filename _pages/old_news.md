# 2025
* [dec 25] Paul Grimal has defended his thesis on *Text-Image Alignment of Visual Generative Models*, dealing with text-to-image generative models. Two of his contribution were accepted as oral at WACV 2024 and AAAI 2026, while a third one is under review (a preliminary version being patended in 2025). Nevertheless, the excellence of the scientific work associated with the thesis only partially reflects the pleasure I had in accompanying Paul for the last three years. His professionalism, reliability and remarkable ability to overcome difficulties and constantly deepen his understanding of the field have made him a scientist of the highest calibre, with whom working on a daily basis is a constantly renewed pleasure. Congratulation Dr. Grimal, the team that will welcome you is very lucky!
* [dec 25] Saad Lahlali has defended his thesis on *weakly supervised methods for 3D point clouds*, dealing with 3D object discovery and 3D detection. I am deeply moved by the completion of this thesis, after three years of working alongside someone who combines professionalism with extraordinary kindness. In addition to three articles published in A/A* conferences demonstrating the originality and technical soundness of his work, Saad gave a brilliant defence, which was particularly clear and educational. Congratulation Dr Lahlali!
* [nov 25] the main contribution of Paul Grimal's thesis [SAGA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13866) has been accepted as an oral presentation at AAAI 2026. The collaboration with Dr Mickaël Soumm was decisive in terms of the theoretical aspects. Congratulation to both of them! 
* [oct 25] during his Master internship, Biruk Ambaw collaborated with Frederic Lin and [their paper](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/121609) has just been accepted to NeurIPS 2025 (Datasets & Benchmarks Track). 
* [sep 25] the article of Saad Lahlali and Alexandre Fournier-Montgieux on weakly supervised 3D object detection [MVAT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07507) has been accepted to WACV 2026. Third top-tier conference for Saad: the PhD defense is expected for next december...
* [mar 25] our [article](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15022) has been accepted to CVPR 2025. It extends the work of (Dr) Sandra Kara on object discovery in images to the case of 3D point clouds, which is the playground of Saad Lahlali. 

# 2024
* [dec. 24] a [new version of TIAM](https://github.com/CEA-LIST/TIAMv2) has been released. The tool is easier to use and it includes [a standarized benchmark](https://github.com/CEA-LIST/TIAMv2/blob/release/doc/benchmarking.md) to compare your visual language model (VLM) to previous ones.
* [dec. 24] Omar Adjali has a new paper accepted at ECIR 2025. With its previous EMNLP'24 article, they were the last papers submitted within the context of the [Meerqat project](https://www.meerqat.fr/)
* [nov. 24] Alexandre Fournier-Montgieux and Saad Lahlali have (each) their papers accepted to WACV 2025. Congrat to them and (Dr) Mickael Soumm!
